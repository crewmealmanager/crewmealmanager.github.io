Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Crew Meal Manager helps cabin crew plan Economy meal quantities across meal carts. This page explains what information the app keeps and why.

What we collect

You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. With Google, we receive your email address and a Google account identifier. With email, we store your email address; your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our sign-in provider and we never see it. We use these only to sign you in and keep your flights linked to your account.

We store the flight information you enter: flight numbers, dates, meal names and quantities, cart names and special meal quantities. We do not ask for or store passenger names, and you should not enter any.

We also record when your free trial started and ends and whether you have a subscription.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, and a copy is kept in your own browser so the app works offline. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, and by Google if you choose Google sign-in.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information, show ads, or share your data with anyone except the services needed to run the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete any flight in the app. To delete your account and all its data, email us and we will remove it.
